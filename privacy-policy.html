Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The Zeus Labs ("we", "us") built Tillora as a point-of-sale app for small and medium businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what data Tillora collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your business profile, products, receipts, customer and supplier balances, expenses and reports are stored locally on your device using a local database. On the free plan, and whenever you use Tillora without an account, this data stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

Account Data

If you create an account, we use Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to create it, identified by your email address. We send a link to that address once, to confirm it is yours; until it is confirmed, the cloud features below stay off.

If you subscribe to Premium, we also keep a record of your subscription status (whether it is active, when it expires, and the Google Play purchase token) linked to your account, so Premium can be confirmed with Google Play and unlocked on your devices.

Shop Data in the Cloud (Premium)

With Premium, your shop's records are kept in Firebase Firestore (a Google cloud database) so that all of your phones, and the managers and cashiers you add, see the same shop. This includes your shop details, product catalog and stock, receipts and returns (including who rang each sale up), customer and supplier accounts with their names and phone numbers and the amounts owed and paid, expenses, stock purchases and write-offs, and day closings. Your shop logo and any receipt photos you attach to a ledger entry are not uploaded; they stay on the device where you added them.

We do not access, read, or sell this data ourselves; it exists solely so your own shop works across your own devices and team.

Team Accounts (Premium)

When you add a manager or cashier, we store their membership in your shop: the name you or they enter, their role, and when they joined. A manager signs in with their own email account. A cashier joins with a one-time code and is given an anonymous account on that phone, with no email or other personal details. Access is enforced on our servers: a cashier can only record sales and returns and can never read your balances, expenses or reports. When you remove someone, or they leave, their access ends at once and the shop's data on their phone is deleted.

Payments

Subscription purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment card details — Google handles this entirely under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

Free-tier use of Tillora shows ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to show relevant ads and measure performance, under Google's own advertising and privacy policies. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) to collect crash reports and diagnostic information (device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash) so we can fix problems. No account details are attached to these reports.

Device Permissions

Camera — used only to scan barcodes/QR codes when you choose to; images are not stored or transmitted.
Bluetooth — used only to connect to a thermal receipt printer you select.
Storage/Photos — used only if you choose to attach a shop logo or a ledger receipt photo; these stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the service providers named above (Google, for Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Play Billing, and AdMob), each governed by their own privacy policies, strictly to provide the features described.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. If you have an account, you can delete it from inside the app (Settings → Delete everything → Delete this shop), which removes your account, the shop's data on that device, and everything stored for it in our cloud database. You can also request deletion by email — see our account deletion page.

Children's Privacy

Tillora is a business tool not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
