Delete Your Tillora Account & Data

Tillora, published by The Zeus Labs

Most of your Tillora data (products, receipts, ledger, reports) is stored only on your own device and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app or clear its storage from your phone's Settings.

This page covers the separate case: your Shop Account (used for Multi-Device Sync) and any data synced to our cloud (Firebase).

How to request deletion

Send an email to contact.zeuslabs@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Tillora Shop Account, with the subject line "Delete my Tillora account".

What gets deleted

  • Your Shop Account (Firebase Authentication record)
  • Your synced product catalog stored in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore)

What is not affected

  • Data stored locally on your own device is unaffected — you control that directly by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Any subscription billed through Google Play must be cancelled separately, through the Google Play Store (deleting your Tillora account does not cancel a Play subscription).

How long it takes

We will process your request and confirm by email within 30 days.
